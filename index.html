The Cheetah

The cheetah is the fastest land animal, capable of reaching speeds up to 70 mph in short bursts. They inhabit a variety of habitats, mainly across eastern and southern Africa. Unlike most other big cats, cheetahs hunt during the day.

Interesting Facts

  • Habitat: Open grasslands and dry forests across sub-Saharan Africa.
  • Diet: Carnivorous, primarily hunting small to medium-sized ungulates like gazelles.
  • Speed: Can accelerate from 0 to 60 mph in just three seconds.
